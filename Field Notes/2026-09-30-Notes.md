## What happened (workflow)

- Downloaded a capture from malware-traffic-analysis.net.
- Briefly analyzed the pcap to find the victim machine (need its IP for the next step).
- Ran the script to convert the pcap to a soundscape.
- Command:
  ```
  python3 netscape.py ~/malware-pcaps/2026-02-28-traffic-analysis-exercise.pcap -m mappings/forest.json -o malware02_forest.wav --target 10.2.28.2 --speed=15
  ```
- Flags:
  - `-m` chooses the mapping
  - `-o` names the output file
  - `--target` is the victim IP (its outgoing traffic, including C2 beaconing, plays right; incoming plays left)
  - `--speed` compresses the 20+ minute capture to avoid the out-of-memory error my current setup hits at real time
